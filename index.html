Welcome to Casa White

Hacienda White, Alamein · North Coast, Egypt

A 170-room hotel inside Hacienda White on Egypt's North Coast, about 900 metres from the sea. Natural timber, textured plaster and stone tones shape the rooms. Two restaurants, a private beach, and golf cars and a shuttle bus running around the clock.
